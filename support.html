Common questions

How do AI features work? Cue's AI is built in - no setup or API key needed. Talk to it in Ask mode to understand your schedule, or Plan mode to add, move, and time-block tasks; it can also read photos of handwritten lists. Free accounts get a limited number of AI actions per day; Premium makes it unlimited. Without AI, Cue still works fully as a manual to-do and time-blocking app.

How do I manage my subscription? Subscriptions are handled by the App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the paywall in the app and tap "Restore purchases".
