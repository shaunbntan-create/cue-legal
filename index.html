Cue - Privacy Policy

Effective July 18, 2026

Cue ("the app") is a local-first to-do and scheduling app. It was built so your data stays on your device. We do not run servers, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Your tasks and notes

Everything you create in Cue - tasks, subtasks, categories, notes, schedules, greetings, and background photos - is stored in a local database on your device only. It is never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Voice input

Voice capture uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is transcribed on your iPhone and is not sent to us.

Optional AI features

Cue's AI features (task parsing and chat) work by connecting to an AI provider (Groq) using an API key that you supply and that belongs to you. When you use these features, the text of the relevant tasks or messages is sent to that provider under your own account and is subject to the provider's privacy policy. We never see, receive, or store this data. If you don't add an API key, nothing leaves your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and purchase history - never your name, email, or task content. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of data

Data deletion

Your data lives on your device. Deleting the app removes it. Purchase records held by Apple are governed by Apple's own policies.

Children

Cue is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
