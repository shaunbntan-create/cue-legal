Cue - Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026

Cue ("the app") is a local-first to-do and scheduling app. Your tasks live on your device. There are no accounts and no sign-ups, and we do not sell or share your personal information. Some optional features (described below) send limited data to servers so they can work.

Your tasks and notes

Everything you create in Cue - tasks, subtasks, categories, notes, schedules, time blocks, greetings, saved AI memories, and background photos - is stored in a local database on your device. We do not run a database of your tasks, and deleting the app deletes this data.

AI features (Cue AI)

When you use Cue's AI (voice-to-task parsing, the AI chat, and AI photo reading), the text needed for that request - your message, a compact summary of the relevant tasks, and any saved AI preferences - is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI relay (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards it to our AI model provider (DeepSeek) to generate the response. This happens only when you actively use an AI feature; the app never uploads your data in the background.

  • Requests include a random device identifier (not your name or any account) used only for daily rate limits and abuse prevention, plus an Apple App Attest check that proves the request comes from the real app.
  • Our relay does not store the content of your requests or responses.
  • The AI provider processes the text to produce the reply, subject to the DeepSeek privacy policy.

Photo reading (OCR)

If you attach a photo in the AI chat (for example a handwritten list), the image is sent to our relay and read by a vision model running on Cloudflare Workers AI to extract its text, which is then used to suggest tasks. Images are processed for that request only; our relay does not store them. If the cloud reader is unavailable, the app falls back to reading the photo entirely on your device.

Voice input

Voice capture uses Apple's speech recognition. Cue asks for on-device transcription first; if that fails, it may retry using Apple's speech service, in which case the audio is handled under Apple's privacy policy. Cue itself never receives or stores your audio.

Calendar and notifications

If you allow calendar access, Cue can mirror your scheduled tasks into your device calendar; those events stay in your calendar account and are never sent to us. Reminders use local notifications generated on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and purchase history - never your name, email, or task content. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No analytics or usage tracking SDKs
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of data
  • No background uploading - data leaves your device only when you use an AI feature, and only what that request needs

Data deletion

Your data lives on your device, so deleting the app removes it. Our AI relay keeps no store of your content to delete. Purchase records held by Apple are governed by Apple's own policies.

Children

Cue is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
